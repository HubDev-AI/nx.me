## Brand Identity

**NXME** is an AI-powered glow-up platform. Users upload a selfie, get precise
face analysis (468 facial landmarks, face shape classification), receive
personalized style recommendations, and see realistic before/after AI-generated
transformations showing how those recommendations look on them.

**NXME is NOT a beauty filter.** We never smooth skin, remove freckles, or
retouch. We analyze bone structure and recommend styling changes (hair, makeup,
accessories, grooming) that work with the user's unique features.

**Core differentiator:** Science-backed style analysis, not AI-generated
prettification. Real recommendations, real transformations.

## Target Audience

- **Age:** 16-35
- **Gender:** All (skews female 65/35 but male glow-up is a growing niche)
- **Location:** United States (primary), English-speaking markets
- **Interests:** Beauty, skincare, self-improvement, fashion, glow-ups, face analysis
- **Platforms they live on:** TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts
- **Psychographics:** Curious about their appearance, want to look their best but
  don't know where to start, love before/after content, respond to "find your
  personal style" messaging

## Content Pillars

1. **Education (40%)** — Face shape science, landmark analysis, "did you know your
   bone structure determines..." content. Makes NXME feel authoritative.
2. **Transformation Reveals (30%)** — Before/after glow-ups, style recommendations
   applied, dramatic reveals. The viral engine.
3. **Trend Participation (20%)** — Riding trending sounds, formats, challenges with
   an NXME twist. Algorithm fuel.
4. **UGC-Style / Behind the Scenes (10%)** — Making-of content, founder stories,
   "how we built this," user reactions. Authenticity signal.

## Tone & Voice

- **Confident but not arrogant** — "We know face shapes" not "We're the best"
- **Playful and energetic** — Match TikTok energy, not corporate
- **Never clinical or medical** — "Your bone structure" not "Your facial anatomy"
- **Inclusive** — All face shapes are beautiful; we help you find YOUR best style
- **Curiosity-driven** — Lead with questions, not answers

## Competitors

Analyze these for content patterns, hooks that work, gaps NXME can fill:

- **Lensa AI** — AI avatar/portrait focus, viral in 2022-2023
- **FaceApp** — Age/gender transformation, massive but shallow engagement
- **Perfect Corp / YouCam** — Virtual try-on, beauty tech, more polished
- **Face shape TikTok creators** — Individual creators doing face shape analysis
  (often inaccurate, manual, not tech-powered)

**NXME's edge over all of them:** We don't just generate pretty pictures. We
analyze 468 landmarks, classify face shape with ML, and give actionable style
recommendations backed by data. Then we SHOW you the result.

## Account Context

- **Status:** Pre-launch warmup phase (building algorithm trust)
- **Posting cadence:** 1-2 posts/day during warmup
- **Goal:** Build initial following, establish content identity, warm up
  algorithm before product launch
- **CTA strategy:** Drive to waitlist/bio link during pre-launch

## Scoring Rubrics

### Hooks (1-10)
- **Curiosity gap (2pts):** Does it create an irresistible "I need to know" feeling?
- **Pattern interrupt (2pts):** Would this stop a fast-scrolling thumb?
- **Identity relevance (2pts):** Does the viewer think "this is about ME"?
- **Scroll-stop power (2pts):** Strong visual/textual opening?
- **Brand alignment (2pts):** Does it connect to NXME's positioning?

### Captions + Hashtags (1-10)
- **TikTok SEO (2pts):** Searchable keywords in caption?
- **Hashtag relevance (2pts):** Mix of niche + volume hashtags?
- **CTA clarity (2pts):** Clear next action for the viewer?
- **Character count (2pts):** Optimized for TikTok (under 150 chars ideal)?
- **Tone match (2pts):** Matches brand voice?

### Content Calendar (1-10)
- **Pillar balance (2pts):** Matches the 40/30/20/10 split?
- **Trend timeliness (2pts):** Incorporates current/emerging trends?
- **Production feasibility (2pts):** Can actually be filmed with available resources?
- **Posting cadence (2pts):** Consistent, appropriate for warmup?
- **Variety (2pts):** No two consecutive posts feel the same?

### Trend Analysis (1-10)
- **Trend momentum (2pts):** Is this trend growing, not dying?
- **NXME fit (2pts):** Natural connection to face/style/glow-up?
- **Content angle strength (2pts):** Is the NXME take genuinely interesting?
- **Timeliness (2pts):** Can we produce this before the trend peaks?
- **Differentiation (2pts):** Does our take stand out from others riding this trend?

### Competitor Analysis (1-10)
- **Insight depth (2pts):** Goes beyond surface-level observation?
- **Actionability (2pts):** Specific things NXME can do differently?
- **Adaptation quality (2pts):** NXME-specific version is genuinely good?
- **Differentiation opportunity (2pts):** Highlights where NXME can win?
- **Pattern recognition (2pts):** Identifies repeatable success patterns?
