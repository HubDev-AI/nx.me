# Competitor Landscape — Seed Data for Agent Knowledge

## Direct Competitors

### FaceApp
- 500M+ downloads, established brand
- Known for: aging filter, gender swap, smile filter
- TikTok presence: Massive UGC, mostly entertainment use
- Weakness: Heavy image manipulation, doesn't look real, privacy controversies
- NXME angle: "We don't morph your face — we style you"

### Lensa AI
- Viral in late 2022 with "magic avatars"
- Known for: AI-generated artistic portraits
- TikTok presence: Viral spike then rapid decline
- Weakness: Novelty wore off, avatars don't look like the real person
- NXME angle: "Our results actually look like you, not a cartoon"

### Perfect Corp / YouCam
- Enterprise beauty tech, AR try-on
- Known for: Virtual makeup try-on, partnered with cosmetic brands
- TikTok presence: Moderate, B2B focused
- Weakness: Limited to makeup, no holistic style advice
- NXME angle: "We analyze your bone structure, not just skin tone"

### AI face shape analyzers (various small apps)
- Many exist on TikTok as trend content
- Known for: Simple face shape quiz, often inaccurate
- TikTok presence: High engagement when trending
- Weakness: No actionable recommendations, just a label
- NXME angle: "We don't just tell you your face shape — we show you what to do with it"

## Indirect Competitors

- Pinterest (style inspiration, no personalization)
- ChatGPT/Claude for style advice (no visual component)
- Stylist apps (clothing-focused, ignore face geometry)
- Dating photo optimization services (manual, expensive)
